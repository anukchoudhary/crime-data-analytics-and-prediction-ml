About CrimeVision AI

Crime Data Analytics & Property Crime Risk Prediction

About CrimeVision AI

CrimeVision AI is a machine-learning based property crime analytics and forecasting platform that combines historical crime data, spatial analysis, temporal patterns and predictive modeling into an interactive web dashboard.

Project Goals

• Analyze Historical Crime

Decompose 5,200 multi-year records across 7 Indian states.

• Identify Geographical Hotspots

Apply K-Means clustering and zone risk stratification.

• Understand Temporal Patterns

Analyze diurnal curves across 8 operational 3-hour windows.

• Property-Crime Categories

Focused exclusively on Theft, Burglary, and Vehicle Theft.

• Location/Time Risk Estimation

Empirical multi-factor probability forecasting.

• Privacy-Aware Architecture

Zero individual profiling, suspect scores, or personal PII.

Important Privacy & Ethical Safeguards

CrimeVision AI operates exclusively at the aggregate zone, temporal window, and property-crime category level. It contains zero suspect profiling, no individual risk scoring, no facial recognition, and no collection of names, personal phone numbers, or Aadhaar details.

CrimeVision AI — Crime Data Analytics & Property Crime Risk Prediction
CrimeVision AI provides statistical risk forecasts based on historical data. Predictions are not certainties, do not identify individuals, and should not be used as the sole basis for enforcement decisions.
